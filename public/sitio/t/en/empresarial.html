Frequently asked questions

Everything you need to know before getting started.

What is a corporate web invitation?

It's a one-screen website (to view from a phone or computer) made to order for your event, brand, conference or launch. Your guests open it with a link, confirm attendance with one click and you manage everything from a private dashboard.

How much does it cost and when do I pay?

The custom design has a one-time payment (the price is shown on this page, VAT included). First you tell us your idea from the dashboard, we review it and contact you with the approval. You only pay once the project is approved.

How long does it take to be ready?

Once you've sent everything, the design has been approved and you've paid, the design takes 5 to 10 business days. Then you review it and ask us for any changes you want before publishing it.

What can I customize?

Everything: color palette, typography, logos, images, texts, sections and features. Each invitation takes on your brand's or your event's identity.

What features are included?

It includes everything in the Deluxe plan: RSVP, location and map, calendar, schedule, dress code, menu, table planner and “Find your table”, tickets with arrival check-in, QR album, downloadable list and much more. See the Features.

How long does it stay online?

12 months from the event date. Before it ends we let you know and you can keep it: download it forever or leave it online with no expiry.

Do my guests have to install anything?

No. They open the link from any phone or computer, with no apps or sign-ups.

Can I make changes after publishing?

Yes. From the dashboard you can fix data and texts as many times as you need, even once published. Design changes are quoted separately.

What if I organize many events a year?

Take a look at the professional accounts: one dashboard with all your events, team and wholesale-priced credits.
