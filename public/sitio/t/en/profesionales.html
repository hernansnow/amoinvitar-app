Questions

Frequently asked questions

Everything you need to know before getting started.

What's the difference between Pro and Studio?

Pro has no fixed fee: you buy single credits or packs of 5 and 10, and you can add your logo next to the Amo Invitar footer. Studio costs 99 € a year and includes white label (no Amo Invitar footer, with your logo), a dashboard with your logo and without our brand, up to 10 administrators, your own domain and packs with bigger discounts.

What is Duo?

Duo adds to every event a Platinum Pro QR album, connected to the invitation and the same dashboard: guests upload their photos and videos by scanning a QR code. With Pro Duo you buy packs of 5 and 10 Duo credits with no fixed cost; with Event Studio Duo (149 € a year) you get everything in Studio and Duo packs of 10 to 100 credits with bigger discounts.

What does a credit include?

A credit (32 €) publishes an invitation Deluxe Pro with all the sections and features you want, in any design and event type. A Duo credit also publishes that event's QR album.

What does a credit not include?

It doesn't cover custom design (of any kind), design changes, additional languages or the invitation's paid add-ons. Those are contracted separately: write to us and we'll quote it.

Can my client publish their invitation?

Yes, if you give them admin access. They can't buy or change your account settings, and they don't see anything about your company or Amo Invitar.

How does white label work?

With Studio, the invitation footer carries neither the Amo Invitar brand nor links: you can add your logo and brand colour, a text with your name, or show no footer at all. Your client's dashboard includes your logo and its colour can be customised, without our brand, contact details or access to our site. You set it up once and it applies to all your events.

Can I use my own domain?

With Studio and Event Studio Duo, yes: your invitations come from a domain or subdomain of your own (for example invitations.yourvenue.com). Setup is free with the 50 and 100 credit packs, or a one-time fee of 49 € one time.

Can I move from Pro to Studio later?

Always. From your Pro dashboard you will see the benefits of Studio and Event Studio Duo, and a button to switch whenever you want.

How is a professional account approved?

Each account is reviewed by hand. You fill in the form on this page with your website or social media link, your field and a description of your work, and the team replies by email. It's part of protecting wholesale prices.

Do credits expire?

Yes: 12 months after purchase, if unused. With Studio or Event Studio Duo, if you renew the annual pass before they expire, the leftover credits roll over to the next year.

How do I pay?

With the payment methods of your country. If you need something special, write to us from the form.
