Questions

Frequently asked questions

Everything you need to know before getting started.

How does the QR code for uploading photos work?

When you create your album you get a unique QR code and link. Your guests scan the QR with their phone camera (or tap the link), type their name and upload photos and videos from the browser. Everything arrives instantly in your private album.

Do guests have to download an app or sign up?

No. They scan the QR with their phone camera, type their name and that's it. It works on any iPhone or Android.

Could it be hard for older guests?

No. They just point the phone camera at the QR code and tap the link that appears: no apps, no sign-ups and no passwords. You can also add a button to your digital invitation so they get in with a single tap.

Can I include the album in my digital invitation?

Yes. Besides the QR code, you get a link and a button ready for your invitation. If your invitation is from Amo Invitar!, for a limited time the Albunear Classic QR collaborative album comes as a gift with the Deluxe package.

Can I create my QR code months in advance?

Yes, and we recommend it: that way you have time to print the cards or include the QR in the invitation. The QR code and link don't change, photo uploads open on the day of the event, and the upload and storage days count from that date.

How do I get the QR code to print?

In your dashboard, under the QR tab, you download the QR as a PNG with your album's design or print the ready-made table cards (4 per A6 sheet or an A4 sign). You can print them at home or at a print shop.

Can I customise the design of the QR templates and the album?

Yes. The Free plan uses a generic template. Classic includes designs for any event and the Free-form design, where you choose fonts, colours and your own background. Platinum adds the exclusive Amo Invitar! designs, matching your invitation, and a cover photo.

When can guests upload photos?

From the day of the event and for 5, 10 or 30 days depending on the plan. That way they can also upload the ones they already had saved on their phone.

What if the internet is slow at the venue?

No problem. Photos are optimised on the phone before uploading, so they are lightweight. If the connection drops, the upload resumes by itself when the signal returns, and guests can also upload them later from home during the upload days.

Do photos lose quality?

It depends on the plan: on Platinum they are stored in their original resolution, exactly as they come out of the phone. On Classic they are stored in high resolution (up to 4K), ideal for viewing and printing. So the album loads fast, lightweight versions are also created for the web and the screen.

Can guests see the photos as they're uploaded?

Yes, if you enable it in your dashboard: the album updates in real time and each new photo appears instantly. If you prefer, you can make it so only you can see them.

How are they shown on the venue screen?

From your dashboard you open the live slideshow on a computer or Smart TV connected to the projector. New photos appear on their own, with transitions and the QR code in a corner so more guests can join.

Can I moderate the photos?

Yes. On all plans you can hide or delete any photo from the dashboard. On Classic and Platinum you can also approve each photo before it appears in the album and the slideshow.

Does it include a digital guest book?

Yes, on all plans. Each guest can leave you a message when uploading their photos and they are all saved together in your dashboard.

How do I download all the photos at the end?

On Classic and Platinum, from your dashboard, with the “Download all (ZIP)” button: you take all the photos and videos at once. On the Free plan they're downloaded one by one.

Does it work if the event is already over?

Yes. Create the album and send the link by WhatsApp: your guests can upload the photos they already took from their phone gallery.

Is it safe to share photos with this service?

Yes. The album is private: you can only get in with its link or QR code, it doesn't show up in search engines and photos are stored encrypted in the Cloudflare cloud. You decide whether guests see everyone's photos, each guest can delete their own, and when the period ends they are permanently deleted.

What happens when the album period ends?

We email you a week before so you can download everything. After that it is permanently deleted for privacy.

Stop chasing your guests to send you the photos!

If you want to have your guests' photos without apps that only complicate things for anyone who doesn't get along with technology, and without creating WhatsApp groups, your best option is Albunear's private album.
