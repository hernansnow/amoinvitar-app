Questions

Frequently asked questions

Everything you need to know before getting started.

What's the difference between Pro and Studio?

Pro has no fixed fee: you buy single credits or packs of 5 and 10, and you can add your logo next to the Amo Invitar footer. Studio costs 99 € a year and includes white label (without the Amo Invitar footer), a dashboard without our brand, up to 10 admins, own domain and packs with more discount.

What does a credit include?

A credit (25 €) publishes one invitation of any package, up to Deluxe, and any model: they all cost the same.

What does a credit not include?

It doesn't cover custom design (of any kind), design changes, additional languages or the invitation's paid add-ons. Those are contracted separately: write to us and we'll quote it.

Can my client publish their invitation?

Yes, if you give them admin access. They can't buy or change your account settings, and they don't see anything about your company or Amo Invitar.

How does white label work?

In Studio, the invitation footer carries no Amo Invitar brand or links: you can put your logo, a text with your name, or show no footer at all. You set it once and it applies to all your events. The colours and style of the invitations don't change.

Can I use my own domain?

With Studio, yes: your invitations go out from your own domain or subdomain (for example invitations.yourvenue.com). Setup is free with the 100-credit pack, or 49 € one time.

Can I move from Pro to Studio later?

Always. From your Pro dashboard you'll see the benefits of Studio and a button to switch whenever you want.

How is a professional account approved?

Each account is reviewed by hand. You fill in the form on this page with your website or social media link, your field and a description of your work, and the team replies by email. It's part of protecting wholesale prices.

Do credits expire?

Yes: 12 months after purchase, if unused. With Studio, if you renew the annual pass before they expire, leftover credits carry over to the following year.

Do prices include VAT?

No. Prices exclude VAT: in Spain and the European Union the applicable VAT is added (€25 + VAT, for example).

How do I pay?

With the payment methods of your country, as on the rest of the site. If you need something special, write to us from the form.
