Questions

Frequently asked questions

Everything you need to know before getting started.

What is an Amo Invitar digital invitation?

It's much more than an image to forward: it's a personalised web page for your wedding. Your guests open it from a link, see all the information (date, place, map, schedule, dress code, gifts…), confirm their attendance with one click and take part. You manage everything from a private dashboard.

How do I send it to my guests?

With a link. You share it by WhatsApp, email, Instagram or wherever you prefer. You can send the same link to everyone or a personal link for each guest, with their name and passes.

Do my guests have to download an app or sign up?

No. They open the link in their phone's browser, with no apps or accounts. It works on any iPhone or Android and also on a computer.

How much does it cost and when do I pay?

You pay only once and only when you publish. You can choose the design, customise it and see your finished invitation without paying anything. If you pick a model from the Free plan, you get your link and pay nothing. There are no subscriptions or hidden charges. See plans and pricing.

What does the Free plan include?

Selected models with custom cover, RSVP, message book, calendar, map, dress code and admin dashboard, for up to 35 guests and online for 15 days from the event date. It's ideal for trying it out or for small gatherings.

How do I see who confirmed?

Confirmations arrive in your private dashboard and by email. You see who is coming, with how many passes and what menu they chose, and you download the final list in Excel or PDF.

Can I change anything after publishing?

Yes, as many times as you want. Fix a time, an address or a photo from the dashboard and the change shows instantly on the same link, without sending anything again. The only thing that can’t be changed is the design: you choose it before publishing.

Can I see how it looks before paying?

Yes. You design your invitation with your details and see it finished, with a full preview. You only pay when you decide to publish it.

Can I have a design that's only mine, from scratch?

Yes. Besides the catalogue models, we create a 100% custom design for your wedding, with your style, your colours and your story. Write to us from the contact page and we'll advise you.

Can I send a different link to each guest?

Yes. With the guest list (Premium and Deluxe plans) each person gets a personal link with their name, passes and family group. That way you know exactly who confirmed and avoid extra guests.

How many guests can I have?

On paid plans, as many as you need: there is no limit on guests or sends. The Free plan allows up to 35 guests.

Can I organise each guest's table and the entrance to the event?

Yes, with the Deluxe plan. You set up the tables from the dashboard and each guest sees theirs in their invitation (“Find your table”). You can also issue QR tickets and register each guest's arrival on the day of your wedding.

What is the QR album?

It's a collaborative album from our sister service Albunear: your guests scan a QR code, upload their photos and videos without downloading apps, and everything ends up in a private album for you. With the Deluxe plan we give you the Classic album.

Can I add music, photos and my own video?

Yes. With Premium and Deluxe you can add background music, animated opening, photo gallery and story. You can also open the invitation with your own cover video.

Can I have the invitation in more than one language?

Yes. If you have guests who speak another language, your invitation can be in two or more, and each person picks theirs.

How can I pay?

With the payment methods of your country: credit or debit card, Mercado Pago (Argentina), PayPal, Apple Pay, Google Pay and, depending on the country, bank transfer. Prices are shown in your currency and you can change it from the menu. Payment is secure and encrypted.

How long will my invitation stay online?

It depends on the plan, counted from the event date: 15 days on Free, 3 months on Basic, 6 months on Premium and 12 months on Deluxe. After that, the invitation shows that the event has ended.

Can I start with one plan and upgrade later?

Yes. You can start with one plan and move up to a higher one whenever you want from your dashboard. You only pay the difference.

Is it safe? Can anyone see my invitation?

You decide: it can be open to anyone with the link, personal link only, or protected with a PIN. Your guests' data is private and only you see it. Invitations don't appear in search engines.

What if I need help putting it together?

We're here for you. You can write to us on WhatsApp or from the contactpage, and the dashboard includes step-by-step help. Premium and Deluxe plans have priority support.

Why a digital invitation and not a paper one?

It's faster, cheaper and more sustainable: no paper or printing, it updates instantly if something changes, reaches everyone at once and saves you chasing replies. If you also want something printed, you can add Save The Date cards.

What is a Save The Date?

It's the advance notice for your guests to reserve the date. It can be a card or its own web page, and the Save The Date card is included in the Premium and Deluxe plans.
