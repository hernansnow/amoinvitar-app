COOKIE SETTINGS

1. What are cookies?

They are small files that a website stores in your browser to remember information about your visit: for example, your preferences or which pages you visited.

2. How we ask for your permission

When you first visit you will see a notice with three options of equal weight: Accept, Reject and Settings. Until you choose, only necessary cookies are used, and Google and Meta work in “no consent” mode, without storing analytics or advertising cookies. Your choice is saved for 12 months, and you can change it whenever you want from the button above or the “Cookie settings” link in the footer.

The demos and invitations don’t show the notice so as not to cover the design, but they respect the choice you made on the website; if you didn’t choose, they behave as if you had rejected.

4. International transfers

Google and Meta may process data in the United States, with the safeguards of the EU-US Data Privacy Framework and standard contractual clauses.

5. How to delete them from your browser

Besides using “Cookie settings”, you can delete or block cookies from your browser:

  • Chrome: Settings › Privacy and security › Third-party cookies.
  • Safari (Mac, iPhone and iPad): Settings › Safari › Advanced › Website Data.
  • Firefox: Settings › Privacy & Security › Cookies and Site Data.
  • Edge: Settings › Cookies and site permissions.

If you block all cookies, some features (such as forms) may not work.
