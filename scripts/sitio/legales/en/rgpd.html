We want you to have full control over your personal data. Here we explain in simple terms what rights you have and how you can exercise them with Amo Invitar!.

1. Who handles your requests

HERNAN JORGE NIEVES, of Amo Invitar!. You can write to him directly about anything related to your privacy at hola@amoinvitar.com.

2. Your rights

  • Access: to know whether we process your data, which data and for what purpose.
  • Rectification: to correct inaccurate or incomplete data.
  • Erasure: to ask us to delete your data when it is no longer necessary or you withdraw your consent.
  • Objection: to object to processing based on our legitimate interest or to receiving commercial communications.
  • Restriction: to ask us to keep your data without using it, for example while a complaint is being resolved.
  • Portability: to receive your data in a structured, commonly used format, or to have us send it to another controller.
  • Withdraw your consent at any time, without affecting what was done before. For cookies, from Cookie settings.
  • Not to be subject to automated decisions: we do not make decisions about you based solely on automated processing.

3. How to exercise these rights

Send an email to hola@amoinvitar.com with the subject “GDPR rights request”, stating:

  • Your name and the email or phone number you contacted us with.
  • The right you want to exercise and, if it concerns a specific invitation, which one.

We will only ask you for a document proving your identity if we have reasonable doubts that it is you. Exercising your rights is free of charge.

4. What if you were invited to an event?

If you RSVP’d or uploaded photos in an invitation made by Amo Invitar!, the controller of that data is the event organizer and we process it on their behalf. You can contact the organizer or write to us: we will pass on your request and, where appropriate, delete your answers.

5. Response time

We will reply within one month at most, which may be extended by two further months in complex cases; we will let you know if that happens.

6. Complaints to the authority

We will do our best to resolve your questions directly. However, if you feel we have not handled your request properly, you have every right to lodge a complaint with the official data protection authorities. More information in our Privacy policy.
