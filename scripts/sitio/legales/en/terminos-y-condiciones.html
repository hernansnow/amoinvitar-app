3. How to purchase

  1. You choose the design and personalize it in the dashboard with your details, texts and photos; you can see it finished without paying.

If the plan is Free, it is published immediately without paying or entering a card (as long as no paid add-on or section from a higher plan has been added).

  4. For custom designs, the work is agreed and approved with the team before starting.

5. Delivery times

Catalog invitations are available to send to guests immediately after you have personalized them to your liking, requested the link and paid for the plan (the free plan does not require payment to get the link and publish the invitation). For design changes to catalog invitations, since the design team carries them out following your instructions, it may take 2 to 4 business days to be ready. For a personalized custom design or corporate invitation, the first version based on your instructions is delivered within 5 to 8 business days, depending on demand, counted from when we receive all the necessary details and materials. During the design process we may need to contact you with questions, and you can request a video call or contact with the design team as many times as you need. Delays due to missing information, details or images, or in replies to revisions, extend the deadline accordingly. Personalized custom designs include 3 design revisions. If you need more design changes, please ask us.

The invitation’s design model is chosen before publishing and paying: once published, it can no longer be changed. 8. Publication and duration

The invitation is published at its own web address and remains online, counted from the date of the event: 15 days on the Free plan, 3 months on Basic, 6 months on Premium and 12 months on Deluxe and custom designs. We will email you before it expires.

To keep it, you can purchase from the Keepsake tab of your dashboard: a PDF with a static view, the downloadable web page (a folder that opens in your browser offline; features that require internet, such as RSVP, the album or interactive maps, do not work offline) or unlimited online time, which keeps your link active for as long as the service exists. After expiry without these options, we may deactivate the invitation and delete guest data in accordance with the Privacy policy. If you want to keep the RSVPs, download them beforehand from your dashboard.
