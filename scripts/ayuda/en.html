1. Getting started

In the store you choose the type of event and the design. Preview shows the sample invitation on a phone; Customize lets you enter the names and date and opens the dashboard. You don’t pay anything until you get the link.

2. The dashboard screen

On a phone

The invitation is at the top and the editing panel below. Drag the grey handle to move the split (or use the ⌃ arrow to make the panel full screen). When you move from one step to another, the invitation scrolls to that section by itself.

Top buttons: WhatsApp (ask for help), ? (this guide), eye (see the invitation full screen, as on a phone or a computer) and Get link. At the bottom: Back, the plan with its price and Next.

On a computer

On the left, the steps; in the middle, the panel; on the right, the large preview. Shrink the panel with the ‹ arrow (to see the invitation bigger) and open it again with ›. At the top: the steps, the design (to change it), the plan, help, full screen, phone or computer, and Get my link.

3. Steps and switches

Each step is a section of the invitation and has its own switch (in the menu and above the title, “Show in the invitation”): on, it appears in the invitation; off, the section can’t be edited until you turn it on. Required sections (cover, venue and date, RSVP, closing, language, link) and “Guests and access” have no switch: they are always in the invitation.

Each step shows the plan it belongs to: Basic Premium Deluxe Add-on (and Free on designs that have a free plan).

Everything saves automatically as you type (“Saved ✓” at the top). Each section’s title can be changed: the field shows the default title.

4. Plan and price

The plan depends on what you choose: if you turn on a Premium section, the plan becomes Premium; with a Deluxe one, Deluxe. Some individual features also raise the plan (for example, more than one cover photo or your own questions in the RSVP).

Design changes (other colors or another font) and Additional language are add-ons: they appear at the end of the menu, after a line, and are added separately to the total. Tap the plan to see the summary of everything you chose.

On designs with a free plan you’ll see “Switch to free plan”: only Basic features remain, up to 50 guests, and the invitation stays online until 15 days after the event.

5. Photos and framing

After uploading a photo you choose which part is shown. On a phone, press and hold for a moment before dragging (so that swiping to scroll the panel doesn’t move the framing).

Cover

You see the whole photo with two frames: Phone (vertical) and Computer (horizontal). Dragging the dot moves both at once; dragging a frame moves only that one. “Center” puts them back in the middle.

Opening envelope (Premium and Deluxe): when guests arrive, the invitation opens from a wax-sealed envelope with the initials. If you turn it off, the design’s welcome window appears instead (with the option to enter with or without music).

For kids’ parties, graduations and gender reveals the cover has a second line under the name: the age (“2 Years”), the class (“CLASS OF 2027”) or the reveal phrase. For group graduations, the name is the school’s.

Gallery

Choose how it’s shown (carousel, mosaic, grid or polaroids) and the thumbnail ratio. Up to 20 photos. In the invitation, tapping a photo opens it full size and it can be zoomed.

Sharing photo

In “Link and sharing preview” you upload the photo that appears on WhatsApp or Facebook and frame it right in the preview.

6. Venue and date

For each venue: date, start time, name (you can also include the address) and the Google Maps link. With the link, the map and the “Get directions” button are created automatically. In Google Maps: search for the place, tap Share and Copy link (the address from the browser bar also works).

The “Add to calendar” button uses a typical duration for the event, so you don’t need to enter an end time.

Accommodation and transport: each section’s title is also the title of its window. With “Add instructions” you write a text that appears in the window before the list of hotels or routes (it’s translated with the rest of the texts if there’s an additional language).

7. RSVP and guests

The RSVP closes automatically on the deadline or when the maximum capacity is reached (it counts guests, companions and children). You can ask about dietary restrictions, alcohol, children (how many) and transport, and add your own questions.

With the Guest list, each guest gets a personal link and sees their name, passes and table. When replying they don’t have to type their name: it’s already there, and companions come from their passes. You can fill the list by hand, by pasting rows or with an Excel file (there’s a template to download).

  • Families (their own column): write the members (“John, Anne, Tom”). The family gets a single link and, when replying, marks who is coming and who isn’t.
  • Phones (another column; you can use one, the other or both): WhatsApp goes straight to their number. The example shows the event country’s code; if you type it without a code, it’s added automatically.
  • Contacts: on Android, “Choose from my contacts” adds them from the address book. On iPhone: Contacts app → press and hold a list → Export → upload the .vcf file.
  • Changing the answer: if guests open their link again, they see what they answered and can change it until the deadline. The latest answer counts.

With the list on, the invitation is invitation-only: only guests on the list can get in, each with their personal link (and their PIN, if you chose PIN). Anyone not on the list sees the access screen.

Save The Date and thank-you cards

They’re in “Add-ons”. The card has the same design as the invitation and you edit it directly: tap a text to move it (by dragging), make it bigger or smaller, and use Write to change what it says. The texts come from the invitation, but changes on the card don’t affect it. Reset puts it back as it comes in the design.

The Save The Date can have just text or a photo (frame it by dragging). The thank-you card has a message, the names and a photo. Generate card creates the image: from then on you can download it or share it on WhatsApp as many times as you need, but it can no longer be changed. The Save The Date with the design’s style is included in Premium and Deluxe (in Basic it’s added separately); the photo card (8 designs) and the thank-you card (8 photo designs, for weddings and quinceañeras) can be added on any plan. Each one’s price appears next to the option.

8. Music and playlist

Background music: an MP3 that plays when the invitation opens (you can choose the second it starts at). Suggest songs: a form for guests to suggest songs. Spotify playlist: paste a playlist link and the invitation shows the list with a record that spins while it plays (the background music pauses by itself).

9. Languages

The main language is chosen in Language (with a button to add an additional language) (for Spanish: neutral, Spain, Rioplatense or Central American). With an additional language, each guest sees the invitation in their language and can switch it with the floating button at the top left, which shows each language’s flag (the country is chosen in the “Additional language” step). The same button pauses or resumes the music.

In the “Additional language” step: first choose the language; then Edit texts in… expands the texts so you can adjust them, and Add another language adds another one (up to three). The invitation’s texts are translated automatically; it’s worth reviewing your own texts (for example, your story).

10. Guests and access

In the Guests and access step there’s just one question: Who can get into the invitation?

  • 🔓 Open (Basic): anyone with the link can get in.
  • 🔑 With PIN (Basic): with the link, guests enter a 4-digit PIN, the same for everyone.
  • 👥 Guest list (Premium): each guest gets a personal link with their name, passes and table. Only those on the list get in.
  • 👥🔑 List + PIN (Premium): same as the previous one, and each guest also enters their own PIN.

With either list option, the list to fill in appears below (by hand, with Excel or from contacts). Under the options you see an example of what each guest receives. After a successful entry, the browser remembers it for 90 days. The owners can always see it with View the invitation from Administration.

11. Getting the link and paying

Once published, the button says Update: changes save automatically and Update checks that everything is complete (and charges the difference if something from a higher plan was added).

When you tap Get my link you see the summary: chosen sections, plan, add-ons and total. Choose the payment method (card, Mercado Pago, PayPal or bank transfer, depending on the country) and, if you have one, a coupon. After payment the invitation goes online at its link, you receive the email with access and the invoice, and you can keep editing. Nothing is sent to your guests: you share the link whenever you want (from Administration → Send). After that, the button says Save changes.

If you later turn on a section from a higher plan, you only pay the difference when saving the changes.

12. Saving your progress

Until it’s published, everything you enter is saved in the browser. To continue on another device, it’s best to save it to an account: Save with Google or with your email (from the first step or from help). The store shows the invitations started in that browser so you can continue where you left off.

13. Administration

At the top of the dashboard are Edit invitation and Administration. Once published, the dashboard opens in Administration. On a phone, the menu slides sideways (the › arrow shows the rest). The eye shows the invitation:

  • Summary: the countdown, how many days are left until the list closes, how many guests still have to reply, and the free spots (if there’s a capacity limit).
  • Send: the link, WhatsApp with a message you can change (if there’s a PIN, it’s already in the message; you can also copy it), copy and the QR code. With a guest list there’s no general link (it’s invitation-only): there’s each guest’s personal link, with WhatsApp, copy, QR and “View” (how that guest sees it). Edit guest list and Change setting (who can get in) open in a window, without leaving Administration. You can see who opened their link (and how many times) and who didn’t, with filters to remind only those who never opened it. Send to all opens WhatsApp with each guest’s message, one after another: tap Send, come back, and the next one is ready (they’re marked as sent; you can stop and continue later). In Replies, Remind all does the same with those who haven’t replied. With the reminders box checked, you get an email 7, 3 and 1 day before the deadline if there are guests who haven’t replied.
  • Replies: at the top, the count (Guests, Attending, Not attending and No reply, in passes). On a phone each reply is a card; on a computer, a row. Who’s coming (green), who isn’t (red) and who’s missing (yellow), with the date; filters, search, sorting and WhatsApp reminders. Download as Excel or PDF; with Confirmed only checked, only those attending.
  • Messages: what guests wrote when replying, to read and download.
  • Songs: the suggestions, each with a Spotify shortcut. To move them all to a playlist: “Copy and open TuneMyMusic”, choose “Paste text” as the source and Spotify as the destination.
  • Tables: the seating plan based on the guest list. To move someone to another table, drag them (⠿) to another table (or to “+ New table”), or type the number. A floor plan to view on the phone and print.
  • Find your table (in Tables): a page where each guest types their name and sees their table. Turn it on with the checkbox and print the QR for the venue entrance.
  • Arrivals: on the day of the event. With “Show the entry pass”, each guest who replied has a QR in their invitation to show at the door. The person greeting guests opens the reception link (shared on WhatsApp, with no access to the dashboard): they scan the QR or search by name and mark the arrival; several people can use it at once. The tab shows live who has arrived.
  • Photo album: the Albunear album (it doesn’t appear if you added your own link in the “QR photo album” section). With Deluxe not yet activated, the “Activate and create the Classic album” button turns it on and opens the setup. Deluxe includes the Classic album at no cost: create it with the button and it appears automatically in the invitation; to upgrade to Platinum you only pay the difference. When on, it replaces Instagram Wall.
  • Messages: the featured one rotates; below, the latest messages with who sent them, how many are coming, the song they suggested and whether they’re attending (with many, the list scrolls by itself and can be moved by hand). Message book creates a designed page with all the messages, to save as PDF or print.
  • Access: add your partner (for weddings) and up to 3 administrators (wedding planner, organizer, a relative…). Each one signs in with their email, without a password, and can edit and manage the invitation. Access can be changed by the owner or the couple.

After publishing, the names and date are fixed (they can be changed by asking on WhatsApp); everything else can be corrected until 7 days after the event.
